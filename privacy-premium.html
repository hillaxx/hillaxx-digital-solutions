Privacy & data use

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This notice explains, in plain language, how information may be handled when you browse the Hillaxx Digital Solutions website, contact the business or use the Client Hub. It is an overview, not a statement that every third-party service or technical setting has been independently audited.

1. Information you choose to provide

If you contact Hillaxx through WhatsApp or email, the details you send may include your name, contact details, organisation and project requirements. The Client Hub uses account sign-in and may display account and project information associated with your access. Provide only information relevant to the enquiry or project.

2. Interactive demonstrations

Some portfolio demonstrations are illustrative. Information entered into the website's demonstration forms and dashboards is stored in your current browser session or browser storage for demonstration purposes only; it is not submitted to Hillaxx as a real customer enquiry or business record. Do not enter real customer, payment or confidential information into a demo.

3. Client Hub

The Client Hub uses account-based access and a connected backend service to support sign-in and project-related information. Access is intended to be limited by the account's permissions. Do not share your password or use another person's account. Contact Hillaxx if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

4. How information may be used

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss requested services.
  • To communicate about an agreed project, its progress and handover.
  • To provide account and project information through the Client Hub.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the website and its demonstrations.

5. Third-party services and links

The website may link to services such as WhatsApp, email providers, hosting platforms or the Client Hub's authentication/backend provider. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy notices. When you follow an external link, the external provider's practices apply.

6. Sharing and public display

Hillaxx does not intend to publish private enquiry or Client Hub information as portfolio content. A client review, name, image or project detail should only be displayed publicly after the relevant content has been approved for publication. Avoid sending sensitive personal, financial or identity information unless it is genuinely required for an agreed service.

7. Retention, security and your requests

Information may be retained for as long as it is reasonably needed to respond to an enquiry, manage an agreed project, maintain account records or meet applicable obligations. No specific retention period is promised in this notice. Reasonable access controls are used in the Client Hub, but no online service can be guaranteed completely secure. You may contact Hillaxx to ask about information you have provided or to request a correction, subject to applicable requirements.

For privacy questions, contact hillaryk171@gmail.com or WhatsApp +254 746 064 990.

Important: This page is a practical website notice, not legal advice. Before public launch, the business should confirm its actual data flows, storage/retention arrangements, cookie or analytics use, and any legal disclosures required for its operations.
