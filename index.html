Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the right package?

Start with the main outcome you need. Share your business type, users, essential features and any existing platform; a suitable package and scope can then be recommended.

Are the displayed prices final?

They are guide prices and starting points. A written quotation confirms the exact features, content, integrations, milestones, costs and delivery assumptions.

How does payment work?

The standard arrangement is 50% upfront and 50% at handover. The agreement will define the project start, review milestones and handover conditions.

Can I request features outside a package?

Yes. Additional features, pages, integrations and complex workflows can be scoped and priced separately before any extra work is started.

Are hosting, domains and third-party fees included?

Not by default. Domain registration, hosting, subscriptions, payment gateway charges, SMS/email usage and other external expenses are identified separately unless your proposal explicitly includes them.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes. The maintenance options above provide defined levels of checks and support. The exact service depends on platform access, technical setup and agreed service hours.

Can a project be delivered in phases?

Where the requirements allow it, a project can be divided into practical phases with separate deliverables and budgets, agreed in the proposal.

Do you guarantee sales or business results?

No specific commercial result is guaranteed. The work is focused on the agreed design, functionality, quality checks and technical deliverables.
